# 🏰 Chiptunes Fantasy v1.5.10

### Castle Fireworks Across Three Systems

Version 1.5.10 brings the castle-and-fireworks spectacle to Amiga and gives the Atari ST its own dark gothic storm scene. The C64 castle scene receives visual refinements, while the Paula silicon output-stage diagram gets a more compact layout.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🌙 Amiga Castle Fireworks

Meet `AmigaCastleFireworks`, a new Amiga-only background DSE featuring a moonlit Gothic citadel, loch, stone viaduct, and reflections. Fireworks, lightning, and other scene accents respond to musical beats and tension. Its colors are quantized to the Amiga 12-bit OCS palette.

### ⛈️ Atari Enchanted Castle

`AtariEnchantedCastle` is reimagined as a dark Gothic castle beneath a storm-lit sky. The scene uses a strict 16-color Atari ST palette with 9-bit Shifter color steps, precomputed ridge geometry, and beat-graded magical effects.

### 🏰 C64 Castle Fireworks refinements

The C64 scene receives sharper roof contours, cleaner pixel alignment, and updated water reflections. Beat strength and musical tension shape its scene choreography while exact beat events continue to drive discrete triggers.

### 🔧 Paula output-stage visualization

The silicon diagram's filter-response windows are narrower and repositioned, and FIFO status labels are more concise. These are visualization changes; Paula audio processing is unchanged.

## 🎧 What to try

Switch between C64, Amiga, and Atari ST during playback to see each system's own castle scene. Try a track with a steady beat and a clear build to watch the visual effects react to musical intensity.

## Validation note

The current focused test run reports **23 passing and 4 failing tests**. Failures cover Amiga macro-state/tension choreography, Atari beat grading and macro-state choreography, and an Atari ordered-dither fade test that expects `document` in the Node test environment. Treat these notes as a release draft until those failures are resolved or explicitly dispositioned.

## Scope

- Castle scenes remain restricted to their respective system backgrounds.
- The Paula change is a display-layout refinement and does not change audio processing.
- No external runtime dependencies are introduced.
