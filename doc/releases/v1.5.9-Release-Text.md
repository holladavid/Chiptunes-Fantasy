Version 1.5.9 brings a gothic demoscene spectacle to the C64 background catalog with **Castle Fireworks**, elevates the player's visual identity with an interactive **Demoscene Soundchip Crest**, and refines the oscilloscope phosphor engine for **crisp transient tracking** during fast chiptune arpeggios.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🏰 Castlevania-style Castle Fireworks DSE (`C64CastleFireworks`)

A brand-new background DSE crafted strictly within the VIC-II 16-color palette and low-resolution raster math:

- **Gothic fortress architecture:** A central keep with crenellated battlements, high spire with an iron finial, glowing amber lancet windows, arched portcullis with torches, and craggy rock foundations standing flush on the horizon (`floorHorizon = 0.55`).
- **Asymmetric mountain crags without ruler lines:** Jagged cliffs rise above the horizon on the left flank, while rolling hills slope gently on the right, organically framing the castle without any artificial horizontal line.
- **Dracula-style gothic trees:** Bare, gnarled oaks with knobby trunks and skeletal forking branches reach into the twilight sky, accompanied by slender drooping gothic spruces.
- **Beat-synchronized fireworks behind the fortress:** Multi-colored C64 fireworks launch and detonate in the sky *behind* the castle silhouette (`drawSky` → `updateFireworks` → `drawCastle` → `drawForeground`). Bursts feature 16-part radial starbursts, parabolic gravity arcs, spark trails, center flashes, and intense climax barrages.
- **Floor-DSE compatibility:** Because the sky and castle reside strictly at `y <= floorY`, activating any Floor DSE cleanly replaces the foreground landscape without clipping or occluding the castle or sky.

### 👾 Demoscene Soundchip brand crest & UI identity

The classic player header has been redesigned into an interactive demoscene soundchip emblem:

- **Interactive DIP-IC crest:** A 16-pin gold-plated dual in-line package with pin-1 notch, ceramic bevel, twilight raster horizon, and an animated 4-channel equalizer wavebar core.
- **System-specific theme palettes:** Dynamically adapts its glow and trace accents to the active soundchip:
  - **C64:** SID/VIC-II purple-blue with amber accents.
  - **Amiga:** OCS Copper/Boing orange-amber with cyan glow.
  - **Atari ST:** YM2149/SM124 phosphor monochrome green.
- **10-second idle auto-fade:** In Scene-DJ demo mode, the header and crest smoothly fade to transparent after 10 seconds of inactivity to maximize the visual demoscene canvas, restoring instantly upon mouse or touch interaction.
- **Refreshed icon set:** Vector SVG, 192px/512px PWA icons, and Apple touch icons updated with the soundchip crest identity.

### ⚡ Razor-sharp oscilloscope traces & faster phosphor decay

The oscilloscope phosphor simulation has been recalibrated for greater clarity and contrast behind the CRT shadow mask:

- **Fast transient recovery:** Phosphor decay rates increased (`slowFade` from 4.8 to 8.0 s⁻¹ active, $\tau = 125\text{ ms}$; `bloomFade` from 14.5 to 18.0 s⁻¹), eliminating ghosting and smearing during ultra-fast arpeggios, pitch bends, and drum transients.
- **Punchy trace contrast:** Baseline trace alpha raised (`edgeAlpha` = 0.20) and optical bloom tuned to 0.58, keeping waveform peaks crisp and legible even through dense CRT slot-mask filtering.
- **Mathematical documentation:** Formula derivations, time constants, and decay characteristics fully updated in `doc/specs/oscilloscope_deep_dive.md`.

## Scope and known limitations

- `C64CastleFireworks` is registered exclusively for C64 background slots; Amiga and Atari ST background catalogs remain distinct.
- The castle base is locked to `floorHorizon = 0.55` by design for seamless Floor-DSE integration.
- Full CRT shadow mask rendering requires WebGL; CSS scanline fallback remains active if WebGL is unavailable.
- Real-time audio synthesis runs entirely in-browser; low-power mobile devices should keep battery-saver mode disabled for optimal AudioWorklet performance.
