# GitHub Pull Request — v1.6.0

```text
Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.0 — System-Wide DSE Overhaul (Amiga, C64 & Atari ST)

## Summary

This release performs a comprehensive overhaul across **12 system-specific Demo-Scene-Elements (DSEs)** for Amiga, C64, and Atari ST to align them with the current DSE development contract (`doc/concepts/dse_development_rules.md`), eliminate DOM/offscreen/pixel-readback bottlenecks, fix bounds and timing defects, and elevate musical choreography and hardware-authentic demoscene presentation.

## Changes

### 1. Cross-DSE Contract, Determinism & Pipeline Cleanups

- **Pure retro-buffer rendering:** Removed `document.createElement('canvas')`, offscreen blitting, shared module caches, `ctx.globalAlpha`, `ctx.save()`/`ctx.clip()`, `applyOrderedDitherFade`, and `getImageData()`/`putImageData()` readbacks across the updated DSEs.
- **PAL-safe timing & determinism:** Replaced `frameCounter`-driven effects, unscaled smoothing factors, and `Math.random()` calls with clamped `dt` timing, local `stateAge` tracking, modulo-wrapped phases, and instance-owned Xorshift32 PRNGs.
- **Foreground vs. background transitions:** Background DSEs fade via native `#000000` Bayer 4×4 `fillRect` curtains; foreground DSEs (`C64VectorStar`, `AtariDotTorus`, `AtariBobs`, `WireframeMorph`) unfold and retract geometrically without obscuring underlying layers.
- **Graded beats, channels & afterglow:** Separated continuous `beatImpulse[0]` reactions from exact `beatStarted`/`beatCount` discrete switches, wired system-appropriate `smooth[c]` channels (Amiga 4-ch, C64 3-voice SID, Atari 3-ch YM2149), and implemented dedicated `afterglow` transitions.
- **Precomputed hardware LUTs & strict bounds:** Added immutable 12-bit Amiga (4,096 colors), 9-bit Atari (512 colors), and C64 Colodore hex caches plus strict integer `[0..width, 0..height]` clipping across portrait (`1:3`), mobile, desktop, ultrawide (`2560×1080`), and `3:1` viewports.

### 2. Amiga OCS/ECS DSEs

- **`KefrensCheckerboard` (`js/visuals/dse/amiga/kefrens-checkerboard.js`):** Added 4 Copper palettes + *Moonlit Indigo* afterglow, 4 tile pattern families, Copper line-scroll S-curves, specular sun-track, traveling Z-shockwaves, scanline-interlaced horizon dither, and `minDim`-responsive projection.
- **`PaulaSiliconBg` (`js/visuals/dse/amiga/paula-silicon-bg.js`):** Removed offscreen DOM canvases and `PAULA_RASTER_CACHE`; added precomputed octant Moiré rings clipped above the horizon, non-overlapping L-R-R-L DMA columns with 3D bus traces, and `horizonLineColor` bugfix.
- **`AmigaRetroSunset` (`js/visuals/dse/amiga/retro-sunset.js`):** Activated second parallax cloud layer, added aspect-stable tiled mountain silhouettes, climax solar raster slits, clipped seagulls, Xorshift32 leaping fish, odd-height-safe water reflections, and blue-hour dusk afterglow.

### 3. Commodore 64 VIC-II DSEs

- **`ChunkyPlasma` (`js/visuals/dse/c64/chunky-plasma.js`):** Added animated 8×8 PETSCII sub-block dithering, 4 VIC-II Colodore ramps, 4 plasma pattern families, Bayer-dithered night afterglow, and reusable row `Float32Array` buffers.
- **`C64RetroSunset` (`js/visuals/dse/c64/retro-sunset.js`):** Converted sky breathing, sun edge dither, and heat-haze to time-based phases; added ordered sky-band dither seams, climax sun slits, deterministic `$D016` water ripples, and dusk afterglow.
- **`SidSiliconBg` (`js/visuals/dse/c64/sid-silicon-bg.js`):** Added dual adaptive landscape/portrait chip layout, fallback waveform synthesis without `regs`, `sustain=1.0` ADSR guard, and beat-reactive bus/VCF highlights replacing full-screen white flashes.
- **`C64VectorStar` (`js/visuals/dse/c64/vector-star.js`):** Quantized 6510 3-tick stutter to `dt` (`0.06s`), added 4 Y-Raster-Interrupt palettes, 2-pass Z-depth lines, 2-step opaque wireframe ghost trails, vertex sparkles, and safe-area Lissajous orbits.

### 4. Atari ST Shifter DSEs

- **`AtariDotTorus` (`js/visuals/dse/atari/dot-torus.js`):** Fixed 50 Hz PAL Z-axis rotation stagnation, added `1×1` back / `2×2` front depth passes, 4 Shifter palettes, knot/spiral morphs, opaque ghost dots, and *Midnight Steel* afterglow.
- **`AtariBobs` (`js/visuals/dse/atari/lissajous-bobs.js`):** Replaced offscreen DOM sprite canvas with scanline circle LUTs, fixed single-quadrant clamping into symmetric 4-way kaleidoscope mirroring, added 3D chain-wave sizing, and Widescreen/Portrait carrier sweeps.
- **`AtariRetroSunset` (`js/visuals/dse/atari/retro-sunset.js`):** Fixed undefined `cPathLight`/`cPathDark`, `drawClippedLine` endpoint doubling (`x0 + t1 * dx`), and out-of-bounds Torii/shrine/water draws; sculpted curved tapered Bonsai boughs tucked beneath sunlit horizontal foliage pads, drifting petals, and Zen afterglow.
- **`WireframeMorph` (`js/visuals/dse/atari/wireframe-morph.js`):** Calibrated projection bounds, added 4 Shifter themes, 2-pass Z-depth wireframe, opaque ghost trails, and inner-cube dual-shell mode.
- **`YmSiliconBg` (`js/visuals/dse/atari/ym-silicon-bg.js`):** Replaced offscreen canvas, `getImageData`/`putImageData` tears, and blind white strobes with dual adaptive landscape/portrait YM2149F square-wave bays, Log-DAC Copper shockwaves, deterministic LFSR noise rain, and non-blocking Timer-B scanline shifts.
