## Summary

This release introduces the **C64 Castle Fireworks** background DSE, redesigns the brand logo into an interactive **Demoscene Soundchip Crest**, and optimizes oscilloscope phosphor decay for crisp transient tracking.

- Adds `C64CastleFireworks`: a Castlevania-style gothic fortress standing flush on the horizon (`floorHorizon = 0.55`) with asymmetric mountain ridges, Dracula-style silhouetted trees, crescent moon, twilight clouds, and musical beat-synchronized fireworks bursting behind the castle.
- Seamlessly layers with Floor DSEs: the sky and castle reside strictly at `y <= floorY`, so Floor DSEs cleanly replace only the foreground landscape without clipping the fortress or sky.
- Redesigns `#brand-logo` into an interactive DIP soundchip emblem with metallic pins, ceramic body, twilight raster horizon, 4-channel equalizer wavebars, copper raster bar, and DJ status LED badge.
- Adds system-specific theme palettes (C64, Amiga, Atari) and a 10-second idle auto-fade during Scene-DJ demo mode.
- Updates vector SVG and raster PWA application icons with the new soundchip identity.
- Tunes oscilloscope phosphor persistence (`slowFade` 8.0 s⁻¹ active, `bloomFade` 18.0 s⁻¹) and increases trace alpha for razor-sharp transient visibility.
- Curated library remains at **214 directly playable tracks: 73 Amiga, 55 C64, and 86 Atari ST**.

## Changes

### 1. C64 Castle Fireworks background DSE (`C64CastleFireworks`)

- **Castlevania-style gothic architecture:** the central keep features crenellated battlements, a towering spire with an iron cross/needle finial, glowing amber lancet windows, an arched portcullis gate with torches, and craggy rock foundations standing flush on the horizon (`this.floorHorizon = 0.55`).
- **Asymmetric mountain crags without ruler lines:** jagged rocky cliffs rise dramatically above the horizon on the left flank, while terraced hills slope gently on the right, organically framing the castle without any artificial horizontal line.
- **Dracula-style gothic trees:** bare gnarled oaks with knobby trunks and skeletal forking branches reach into the night sky, complemented by slender drooping gothic spruces/pines.
- **Beat-synchronized fireworks behind the fortress:** multi-colored C64 fireworks launch and detonate in the sky *behind* the castle silhouette (`drawSky` → `updateFireworks` → `drawCastle` → `drawForeground`). Bursts feature 16-part radial starbursts, parabolic gravity arcs, spark trails, center flashes, and intense climax barrages.
- **Atmospheric night landscape:** C64 raster twilight bands, crescent moon with dark cutout, twinkling stars, rolling meadows with dithered grass, plowed furrow fields, and a perspective S-curve winding path leading from the portcullis.
- **Floor-DSE compatibility:** because sky and castle occupy `y <= floorY`, activating any Floor DSE cleanly covers the foreground without cutting into the castle or sky.

### 2. Demoscene Soundchip brand crest and UI identity

- **Interactive DIP-IC crest emblem:** redesigned `#brand-logo` features a 16-pin gold-plated DIP soundchip package with orientation notch, ceramic bevel, twilight raster horizon, and an animated 4-channel equalizer wavebar core.
- **Demoscene typography and copper lockup:** dual-deck chrome-cycling typography with an animated copper raster bar and an active DJ status LED badge.
- **System-specific color palettes:** dynamically adapts between C64 (VIC-II/SID purple-blue), Amiga (OCS Copper/Boing orange-amber), and Atari ST (YM2149/SM124 phosphor green).
- **Interactive animations and idle auto-fade:** bouncing wavebars during demo mode, 3D push responses, and an automatic 10-second idle fade-out during Scene-DJ demo mode that restores immediately upon pointer movement.
- **High-resolution icon set:** refreshed SVG, 192px, 512px, and Apple touch icons incorporating the new soundchip mark.

### 3. Oscilloscope phosphor persistence and decay tuning

- **Crisp transient definition:** optimized phosphor decay rates (`slowFade` adjusted from 4.8 to 8.0 s⁻¹ active, `bloomFade` from 14.5 to 18.0 s⁻¹) reduce ghosting trails and visual clutter during rapid chiptune arpeggios and note transitions ($\tau = 125\text{ ms}$).
- **Higher trace contrast:** increased baseline minimum trace alpha (`edgeAlpha` = 0.20) and optical bloom (0.58) ensure sharp, punchy signal visibility through the CRT shadow mask.
- **Documentation:** updated mathematical decay formulas and time constants in `doc/specs/oscilloscope_deep_dive.md`.

## Scope and boundaries

- The castle fireworks DSE is registered exclusively for C64 background use; Amiga and Atari ST setlists are unaffected.
- The castle base is locked to `floorHorizon = 0.55` to ensure 100% interoperability with all existing and future Floor DSEs.
- All visual rendering adheres strictly to C64 VIC-II 16-color palette limitations and integer pixel snapping.
- No external frameworks, heavy libraries, or runtime dependencies were introduced.
- All audio synthesis remains genuine real-time emulation with zero prerecorded shortcuts.
