# 💾 Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.4

### Workspace Modes, Story-First Museum Dossier & Cross-System Library Overhaul

Version 1.6.4 upgrades the right-hand **Living Silicon & Museum workspace**, adds **Scroller-to-Museum cross-linking**, and reorganizes all **216 curated entries** (**215 tracks + 1 SID test suite**) across **Commodore 64**, **Amiga**, and **Atari ST** with rewritten hardware-focused liner notes.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🖥️ Workspace Modes, Museum Sub-Tabs & 1900px Command Deck
- **`[ SPLIT ]` / `[ MUSEUM ]` / `[ CHIP ]` Workspace Modes:** Toggle seamlessly between simultaneous hardware + archive view, full-height museum reading (shortcut `A`), or expanded Living Silicon die visualization (`index.html`, `js/app.js`, `css/style.css`). Living Silicon updates pause automatically while hidden in Museum mode (`js/visuals/visualizer.js`).
- **`[ TRACK STORY ]` & `[ HARDWARE ]` Sub-Tabs:** Organizes track liner notes and collapsible `> BINARY ANALYSIS` in a 2-column `.museum-story-dossier` layout alongside semantic `.museum-hardware-dossier` specs (`js/content/museum.js`), with direct jump links between story and chip architecture.
- **Full-HD 3-Column Command Deck & Scroller Link:** Lowers the 3-column Command Deck threshold from `2200px` to **`1900px`**, adds a sticky `[ ↓ FULL MUSEUM (A) ]` overflow hint in Split mode, and introduces a `MUSEUM ↗` button in `#scroller-zone` (`js/visuals/scroller.js`) with fixed `Historie:` → `SID/Technik/YM2149/Datei & Replay:` excerpt boundaries.

### 📚 Cross-System Library Reorganization & Museum Rewrite (216 Entries)
- **Commodore 64 (57 entries: 56 tracks + 1 test suite):** Unified kebab-case layout across `games/`, `musicdisks/`, `demoscene/`, and `tests/`; adds Rob Hubbard's original *Delta* and Martin Galway's *Ocean Loader 1*; separates Tomas Danko's *Delta* reinterpretation; fixes swapped *Radio Napalm* / *Coma Light 13 (Tune 1)* SIDs & `songlengths.js` timings; renames *Compilation III* and *Comaland 100% – FaceTime*; and rewrites `tracks/c64/museum.js` with DeepSID/CSDb/Demozoo references.
- **Amiga (73 tracks):** Unified kebab-case layout across `games/`, `demoscene/`, and `tracker-xm/`; replaces path-prefix special cases in `tracks/amiga/playlist.js` with explicit metadata flags (`sampleBank`, `envSpec`, `format: 'hip68k'`); removes the unplayable `beast1.title.dw` binary; and rewrites all 73 `tracks/amiga/museum.js` cards.
- **Atari ST (86 tracks):** Unified kebab-case layout across `games/` and `demoscene/`; moves `spherical-intro.ym` and `punish-your-machine` to their proper categories; reunites *Stormlord*; extracts all 86 inline descriptions from `tracks/atari/playlist.js` into a dedicated `tracks/atari/museum.js`; and eliminates virtual `path`/`sourcePath` discrepancies.
