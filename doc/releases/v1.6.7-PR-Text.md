# GitHub Pull Request — v1.6.7

## Pull Request Title

```text
Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.7 — 18 System-Authentic DSEs (C64, Amiga & Atari ST), C64/Atari Floor Layers & DOM-Free Retro Rendering Kit
```

## Summary

Version 1.6.7 expands the Stage-DJ visual roster by **18 new, system-authentic Demo-Scene Elements (DSEs)**—6 per system (**C64**, **Amiga**, **Atari ST**), 2 per layer (`background`, `floor`, `foreground`)—giving both C64 and Atari ST dedicated `floor` visuals for the first time while introducing shared, DOM-free clipping, Bayer-fade, Xorshift32, and bitmap-font utilities, `StageManager`-aligned `1.5 s` transitions, and **21 automated test suites**.

## Changes

### 1. Shared DOM-Free Retro Helpers, Bitmap Font & Hardware Kits (`js/visuals/utils/*`, `js/visuals/dse/amiga/amiga-kit.js`, `js/visuals/dse/atari/atari-kit.js`)

- **`js/visuals/utils/hardware-constraints.js`:**
  - Added `createXorshift32(seed)` deterministic instance PRNG (`next()`, `float()`, `int(n)`, `reset()`).
  - Added `bayerFadeCells(ctx, x, y, w, h, visibility, cell, color)`: DOM-free 4×4 Bayer ordered-dither fade using horizontally run-merged `fillRect` calls with automatic cell scaling under `4000` ops.
  - Added `drawAliasedLineClipped(...)` (Liang-Barsky clip + run-merged Bresenham spans), `fillAliasedPolygonClipped(...)` (scanline fill + clip rectangle), and `drawAliasedEllipseOutlineClipped(...)` (Midpoint ellipse with horizontal/vertical run-merging and clip rectangle).
- **`js/visuals/utils/bitmap-font.js` & `js/visuals/dse/universal/track-presenter.js`:**
  - Extracted the `5×7` bitmap font (`GLYPHS`, `normalizeText`, `measurePixelText`, `truncatePixelText`, `drawPixelText`) from `TrackPresenter` into a shared utility without changing `TrackPresenter` behavior.
  - Precomputed immutable `GLYPH_COL_RUNS`, `GLYPH_ROW_RUNS`, `getGlyphColRuns()`, `getGlyphRowRuns()`, and `drawBitmapRun()` for zero-allocation scroller rendering.
- **`js/visuals/dse/amiga/amiga-kit.js`:**
  - Defines immutable 12-bit OCS/ECS quantized ramps (`MAGMA`, `OCEAN`, `JADE`, `VIOLET`, `GOLD`, `STEEL`), `INK`, `SNOW`, `FAM`, `CH_RAMPS`, `COOL`, `PP6`, `PAULA_LRRL = [0, 3, 1, 2]`, and `TRANSITION_IN = TRANSITION_OUT = 1.5`.
- **`js/visuals/dse/atari/atari-kit.js`:**
  - Defines immutable 9-bit Shifter quantized ramps (`RED5`, `GRN5`, `BLU5`, `AMB5`, `CYN5`, `VIO5` via `st()` max `#fcfcfc`), `INK`, `WHITE`, `FAM`, `WFAM`, `SETS`, `COOL`, `PP5`, and the 14-color luminance-balanced `RING_HUE`.

### 2. Commodore 64 — 6 New DSEs (`js/visuals/dse/c64/*`)

- **`C64RasterTunnel` (`raster-tunnel.js`, `background`):** Aspect-correct nested raster frames anchored at `(width/2, horizon - 1)`, 32-ring SID waterfall `lv[]` latched at far entry, `climax` right-half `+2` split, 6-pulse pool with black inner border, depth-ordered family wipe (`0.6 s`), and `afterglow` locked to `BLUE`.
- **`C64FireWall` (`fire-wall.js`, `background`):** 50 Hz `Uint8Array` heat simulation flush to `horizon`, nearest-neighbor resize resampling, 3 cosine-interpolated SID zones, 2×2 step dither, ember glow/bed, 24-spark `Int16Array` pool, hard-beat fire pillars, and `BLUE` cold-fire `afterglow`.
- **`C64RasterRoad` (`raster-road.js`, `floor`):** Perspective scanline road (`y >= horizon`), 4 terrain families (`Grass`/`Desert`/`Ice`/`Night`), anti-moiré horizon haze dithering, 3 hysteresis-gated SID lanes, unified Z-sorted pylon/gate painter pass (`MAX_PROPS = 13`, `MIN_GATE_Z_GAP = 1.35`), and `climax` streaks (`vEff <= 1.7`).
- **`C64DanceFloor` (`dance-floor.js`, `floor`):** 61×9 perspective Color-RAM tile matrix (`y >= horizon`), 4 beat patterns (*Chase*, *Voices Waterfall*, *3-Slot Ripple*, *Searchlight*), 12.5 Hz SID zone sparkles, `climax` rainbow rows, and 2 Hz beatless clock fallback after `> 1.2 s`.
- **`C64SineScroller` (`sine-scroller.js`, `foreground`):** Shared `5×7` bitmap-font scroller with `1 px` black outline pass, Raster-IRQ band color pass, hysteresis `GREY` echo line, `wide`/`tall` rotation continuity (`scrollPos` preserved), and geometric scroll-in/out transitions.
- **`C64BorderRasters` (`border-rasters.js`, `foreground`):** VIC-II `$D020` border raster bars for 3 SID voices with `1 px` black inner contour, `climax` 16-px diagonal shear stripes, 8-sprite `7×7` border-centerline star pool, and geometric width fade (`btEff <= floor(minDim/4)`).

### 3. Amiga OCS/ECS — 6 New DSEs (`js/visuals/dse/amiga/*`)

- **`AmigaRotoZoomer` (`rotozoomer.js`, `background`):** `<= 6000`-cell run-merged rotozoomer across 4 `64×64` textures, tension-gated kaleidoscope folding (pending rule on beat / `1.2 s`), 4 screen-space `PAULA_LRRL` brightness zones, Copper vignette, Copper beat bands, and vertical wipes.
- **`AmigaTechTech` (`tech-tech.js`, `background`):** Copper `BPLCON1` scanline shifter across 4 run-buffered patterns (`P0..P3`), 3-harmonic wave displacement (`|dx| <= 0.2 * minDim`), adaptive `rowH = 2 | 3` budget protection, 4 vertical Paula amplitude zones, and a sweeping 6-row shockwave.
- **`AmigaVoxelHills` (`voxel-hills.js`, `floor`):** `128×128` periodic-noise height-map raycaster with 2D slope shading, winding canyon trajectory, 3D mountain peaks silhouetted above `horizon` (up to `14%` ceiling with `1 px` `INK` crests and transparent sky), 4 `PAULA_LRRL` waterfall ridges, Z-contour waves, and `> 16 ms` adaptive LOD guard.
- **`AmigaParallaxGround` (`parallax-ground.js`, `floor`):** 8-band parallax ground (`y >= horizon`) with 4 cascading-wipe terrain themes (`JADE`/`STEEL`/`GOLD`/`OCEAN`+`SNOW`, `MAGMA` climax), Paula-band foliage height equalizer, 6-slot `8×10` pixel-art sprites, and 16 gravity dust particles.
- **`AmigaTwister` (`twister.js`, `foreground`):** 1–3 aspect-scaled 4-sided raster bars with 4 morphing twist profiles, `1 px` `INK` silhouettes/seams, and fixed 1:1 Paula channel-to-face binding (`0..3`).
- **`AmigaVectorBalls` (`vector-balls.js`, `foreground`):** Precomputed `Int16Array` shaded ball templates (`r = 2..14`) Z-sorted per body across 4 morphing formations (`26..36` slots), index-activated ball scaling, ultrawide dual counter-rotating bodies, `i % 4` Paula assignments, and `ten > 0.70` ring-buffered ghost trails.

### 4. Atari ST — 6 New DSEs (`js/visuals/dse/atari/*`)

- **`AtariMetaBlobs` (`meta-blobs.js`, `background`):** `<= 5000`-cell metaball field (`3` YM2149 voice blobs + up to `4` orbiting satellites across 4 morphing orbital shapes), long-axis responsive layout, `#fcfcfc` `climax` cores, and Timer-B scanline-split wipes (`<= 14` colors/scanline).
- **`AtariJuliaCycler` (`julia-cycler.js`, `background`):** `<= 6000`-cell Julia fractal morphing across 4 constants (`F0..F3`), 3 radial YM2149 phase zones, strobe-safe `RING_HUE` rotation (`<= 6 steps/s`), and Timer-B scanline-split wipes (`<= 16` colors/scanline).
- **`AtariLaserGrid` (`laser-grid.js`, `floor`):** Perspective laser grid (`y >= horizon`), anti-moiré scanline haze, continuous perspective column spans, 50% scanline-rastered YM2149 lane glows, 4-slot pulse lines, decaying grid sine waves, `climax` laser sweeps, and Timer-B wipes (`<= 6` colors/scanline).
- **`AtariPondRipples` (`pond-ripples.js`, `floor`):** Row-dithered water plane (`y >= horizon`), 24 `Xorshift32` glints, 3 YM2149 wave emitters, deterministic ambient droplets, 12-slot concentric ripple pool via `drawAliasedEllipseOutlineClipped`, 12 spray streaks, and Timer-B wipes (`<= 6` colors/scanline).
- **`AtariFlatShadedTrio` (`flat-shaded-trio.js`, `foreground`):** 6 unit-normalized polyhedra in 4 trios (`MESHSETS`), dual backface culling, Painter Z-sort, deduplicated `1 px` `INK` edge pass, face explosion, `0.40 s` geometric mesh swaps, and Timer-B wipes (`<= 16` colors/scanline).
- **`AtariLineRibbons` (`line-ribbons.js`, `foreground`):** 3 YM2149 Bresenham ribbons (`4` vertices, `5` in `climax`), `25 Hz` 8-slot history ring buffer + `snapNow`, `(+1,+1)` `INK` contrast shadow + double head, proportional vertex/history resize remapping (`<= 1 px`), and Timer-B wipes (`<= 13` colors/scanline).
