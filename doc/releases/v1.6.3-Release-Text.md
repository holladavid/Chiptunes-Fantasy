# 💾 Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.3

### C64 "Blade Runner" Director's Cut Core, Complementary bs2b & O(1) Limiter, Cross-System Engine Hardening

Version 1.6.3 completes the Blade Runner DSP series with the **C64 MOS 6581/8580 "Blade Runner" Core v1.1**, upgrades the **Master Bus DSP**, and hardens worklet transport, seeking, and AudioContext lifecycle across all three systems.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🎛️ C64 MOS 6581/8580 "Blade Runner" Core v1.1 (Director's Cut)
- **1 MHz Lockstep + 3× Yamaha CS-80 Dual-Rank Voices:** Pairs a cycle-stepped 6502 + Kaiser-Sinc stereo SID core (with Tow-Thomas-accurate `-bp` / `$5x` notch topology, subtle `3OFF` AC crosstalk, live `$D41B/$D41C` updates, and `dcBaseline` start-jump cancellation preserving `$D418` PCM digis) with three parallel CS-80 PolyBLEP voices.
- **6502 Bus-Settle Latch & `$D418` Onset Designer:** Eliminates torn 16-bit frequencies and false arpeggios via a 64-cycle settle latch and sample-domain timing, applies selective `$D417` filter coupling and soft `$11` triangle-flute scaling, and replaces continuous 48 Hz sine droning on speech samples with a dual-envelope `74 → 45 Hz` onset sub-kick.
- **Studio Space & 1 MHz Optimization:** Features dynamic Hz/waveform voice staging, bass-exempt **Dimension-D BBD Chorus**, fluttered **Multi-Tap Tape Delay**, **19 ms pre-delay + prime-length 4-channel Lexicon 224 FDN** with orthogonal mono-compatible stereo taps, a $C^\infty$ bias-shifted tape saturator, hoisted filter coefficients, Padé-[5/4] `fastTanh`, and Zero-GC state reuse.
- **Technical Book:** Deep-dive documentation added in `doc/books/SID-BLADERUNNER-DSP.md`.

### 🎧 Master Bus DSP Upgrade (`master-processor.js`)
- **Complementary bs2b Bauer Crossfeed:** 700 Hz crossfeed low-pass + 1000 Hz direct shelf with `0 dB` DC mono sum and a click-free 10 ms toggle crossfade.
- **Smoothed O(1) 1.0 ms Lookahead Brickwall Limiter:** Monotonic sliding-window minimum deque with 1.0 ms moving-average attack smoothing and periodic drift compensation at `-0.35 dBFS`.

### 🛠️ Cross-System Worklets, Engine & UI Hardening
- **C64 (`sid-exact`, `sid-fantasy`):** Sample-rate-scaled 10 ms fade ramps, opt-in `SET_DIAGNOSTICS`, $C^1$ soft-clipping knee at `±0.95`, DC blocker resets, and accurate register-rate & final-stop telemetry.
- **Amiga (`paula-exact`, `paula-fantasy`):** `CLEAR_SAMPLES` cleanup, low-byte DMA tail completion, 20 kHz FIR stopband, variable-pattern `SEEK_TRACK`, tick-0 `basePeriod` restoration, exact $2^{-n/12}$ arpeggio intervals, clamped `0x09` sample offsets, sample-rate-scaled Schroeder/Moorer reverb with corrected allpass phase, and pause state preservation.
- **Atari ST (`ym-bladerunner`, `ym-exact`, `ym-fantasy`, `ym-standard`):** Consistent 10 ms anti-click fades, `playerRate` & `loopFrame` playback across all cores, fixed power-of-2 delay masking & sample-rate scaling in `ym-fantasy`, and clean `PLAY_TRACK`/`SEEK_TRACK` resets.
- **Audio Engine & App:** Full AudioContext/worklet teardown & async load-token race guards, iOS `'interrupted'` context resume, `MediaSessionController` cleanup & shared frame-time helpers, pointer scrubbing, and Amiga sample / SID temperature persistence across core switches.
