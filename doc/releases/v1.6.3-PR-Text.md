## Summary

This release introduces the new **C64 MOS 6581/8580 "Blade Runner" Core v1.1 (`sid-bladerunner`)** along with its mathematical/DSP reference book (`doc/books/SID-BLADERUNNER-DSP.md`), upgrades **`master-processor.js`** with a complementary **bs2b Bauer Crossfeed** and an **O(1) smoothed 1.0 ms lookahead brickwall limiter**, and executes a comprehensive **lifecycle, transport, sample-rate, and tracker-accuracy hardening pass** across C64, Amiga, Atari ST worklets, `audio-controller.js`, `media-session.js`, and `app.js`.

## Changes

### 1. C64 MOS 6581/8580 "Blade Runner" Core v1.1 (`js/worklets/c64/sid-bladerunner.js`, `js/worklets/registry.js`, `doc/books/SID-BLADERUNNER-DSP.md`)

- **Registered new C64 processor (`sid-bladerunner-processor`):** Added `SID BLADE RUNNER` to `workletRegistry.c64` and `livingSiliconCoreTitles` (`MOS 6581 · CS-80 BLADE RUNNER`).
- **Cycle-accurate 1 MHz SID core & Tow-Thomas ZDF-SVF:**
  - Runs 6502 CPU and stereo SID in 1 MHz PAL lockstep with 256-phase Kaiser-Sinc resampling and 2× oversampled TPT-SVF.
  - Fixes `$5x` (`LP+HP`) notch topology and inverts `BP` (`-bp`) to match real MOS Tow-Thomas integrator phase; replaces unipolar `3OFF` DC pops with subtle AC crosstalk (`voice2 * 0.006`), keeps `$D41B/$D41C` (`regs[27..28]`) synced every cycle, and cancels the initial `+1.05` master DC step via `dcBaseline` while preserving 100% of `$D418` 4-bit PCM digis.
  - Hoists `Math.tan()` and divisions into `updateFilterParameters()`, uses a monotonic Padé-[5/4] `fastTanh` approximation, and reuses SID state via `reset()` for zero GC pressure.
- **6502 bus-settle latch & CS-80 3-voice dual-rank layer (`CS80SidVoice`):**
  - Adds a 64-cycle register-settle window (`settleMax = 128`) and pure audio-sample-domain timing to prevent torn 16-bit frequencies and false arpeggio detection between `STA $D404` and `STA $D400/$D401`.
  - Applies `$D417` per-voice filter routing (`sidCutoffNorm` LPF modulation and `$40` HPF boost), preserves soft `$11` triangle-flute harmonics (`0.36×` pre-VCF scale), uses centered symmetric dual-rank detuning (`phaseSub` on exact SID center), and adds a `<-114 dBFS` idle sleep fast-path.
- **`$D418` onset transient designer & studio stereo space:**
  - Distinguishes >400 Hz PCM digi streams from 50 Hz volume fades and uses a fast/slow dual-envelope onset detector + 95 ms holdoff to drive a `74 → 45 Hz` sub-kick and CS-80 sidechain dip without droning during speech/sustained samples.
  - Adds dynamic Hz/waveform voice staging, bass-exempt **Dimension-D BBD Chorus**, flutter-modulated **Tape Multi-Tap Delay**, **19 ms pre-delay + prime-length 2× Allpass + 4-channel quadrature-modulated Lexicon 224 FDN** with orthogonal mono-compatible stereo taps (`cL · cR = 0`), and a $C^\infty$ bias-shifted master tape saturator.
- **Documentation:** Added `doc/books/SID-BLADERUNNER-DSP.md` covering the signal flow, Tow-Thomas/TPT notch proof, 6502 bus-settle concurrency, `$D418` onset detection, orthogonal FDN math, and Padé-[5/4] optimization.

### 2. Master Bus DSP Upgrade (`js/worklets/lib/master-processor.js`)

- **Complementary bs2b Bauer Crossfeed:** Replaced the fixed-delay crossfeed with a complementary 700 Hz crossfeed low-pass + 1000 Hz direct high-shelf topology (`0 dB` DC mono sum, `-4.73 dB` cross-feed) with 10 ms smooth crossfade (`crossfeedMix`) and filter-state reset on activation.
- **Smoothed O(1) Lookahead Brickwall Limiter:** Replaced sample-by-sample step attenuation with a power-of-2 lookahead buffer, O(1) monotonic sliding-window minimum deque, 1.0 ms moving-average attack smoothing over the lookahead window, periodic sum-drift reset, and mono fold-down support.

### 3. Cross-System AudioWorklet Hardening (`c64/`, `amiga/`, `atari/`)

- **C64 (`sid-exact.js`, `sid-fantasy.js`):** Standardized 10 ms sample-rate-scaled `fadeStep`, gated 1 Hz lab logging behind opt-in `SET_DIAGNOSTICS`, added a $C^1$-continuous soft-clipping knee above `±0.95` in `sid-exact.js`, reset `dcBlockL/R` on track/subsong load in `sid-fantasy.js`, computed register write rate from actual `visSamplesAccum / sampleRate`, flushed final `isPlaying: 0` telemetry via `wasPlaying`, and added mono fold-down.
- **Amiga (`paula-exact.js`, `paula-fantasy.js`):**
  - Added `CLEAR_SAMPLES` handler, variable-pattern-length `SEEK_TRACK` calculation, tick-0 `basePeriod` restoration, post-sample `period === 97` note-off ordering, and clamped pattern-break targets.
  - `paula-exact.js`: Added `stopAfterLowByte` so one-shot DMA finishes its trailing low byte cleanly, tightened `FIR_TAPS` cutoff to `20 kHz`, added constant-power `cos/sin` XM panning, and clamped `0x08` XM panning.
  - `paula-fantasy.js`: Scaled `StudioStereoReverb` comb/allpass delays to `sampleRate` with full `reset()`, fixed the Schroeder allpass formula (`-apFeedback * diff + delayed * (1 - g^2)`), preserved paused channel/COSO-VM state on `STOP_TRACK`, fixed arpeggio intervals to exact `2^(-n/12)`, clamped `0x09` sample offsets, and synced `basePeriod` on slides.
- **Atari ST (`ym-bladerunner.js`, `ym-exact.js`, `ym-fantasy.js`, `ym-standard.js`):** Standardized 10 ms sample-rate-scaled anti-click fade ramps, `playerRate` and `loopFrame` handling, clean state resets (`PLAY_TRACK`/`SEEK_TRACK`), and mono fold-down; fixed `ym-fantasy.js` delay buffer masking (`nextPow2`) and sample-rate-scaled coefficients; unified `ym-standard.js` DC blocking with `DCBlocker(sampleRate, 20.0)`.

### 4. Audio Engine, Media Session & App Integration (`js/audio/audio-controller.js`, `js/audio/media-session.js`, `js/app.js`)

- **`audio-controller.js`:** Added `teardownWorkletNode` & `teardownAudioEngine` cleanup, per-system `coreLoadTokens` against async races, atomic `initAudioEngine` setup with error cleanup, explicit stereo `outputChannelCount: [2]` routing, initial crossfeed sync, and iOS `'interrupted'` `AudioContext.resume()` handling.
- **`media-session.js`:** Hardened `setMetadata`, `setSeekable`, and `clear()` (`setPositionState(null)`), added `destroy()`, and exported shared `DEFAULT_PLAYER_RATE`, `getPlayerRate`, `framesToSeconds`, `secondsToFrames`, `formatFrameTime`, and `isStoredLoopTransition` helpers.
- **`app.js`:** Added double-boot guard (`hasBooted`) and init error state, pointer-event progress scrubbing, `isTransitioning` and `playRequestToken` guards during track/core switches, `loadedAmigaSamples` and `lastSidTemperature` persistence across core reloads, `startSong` preservation on C64 playback start, `livingSiliconCoreTitles` updates for Blade Runner & Fantasy cores, and `resumePlayback()` alignment on transport actions.
