# 🕹️ Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.1

### Stage Cockpit Polish, Rock-Solid 50 Hz PAL Choreography & Refined Amiga Visuals

Version 1.6.1 follows the v1.6.0 DSE Overhaul with targeted fixes across the **responsive UI & fullscreen stage**, the **Scene-DJ timing and lifecycle engine**, and three **Amiga OCS Demo-Scene-Elements**.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🖥️ Responsive Command Deck, Fullscreen Cockpit & Pixel-Sharp Canvas
- **Unified fullscreen controls & seeking:** Both the desktop `#now-playing` overlay and the mobile `#fs-ui-bar` cockpit now support pointer progress scrubbing on seekable Amiga and Atari tracks, display subsong badges (`[SUB x/y]`), standardize the fullscreen button to `[ FULL ]` / `[ EXIT ]`, and eliminate bottom borders and Safari C64 theme bleed in fullscreen.
- **Balanced desktop, widescreen & mobile layouts:** Restored clean `#player-panel` markup, harmonized typography tokens across C64, Amiga, and Atari ST, restricted the 3-column Command Deck to `>= 2200px` widescreen while keeping a 2-column layout up to `2199px`, positioned open `.silicon-guide` panels next to the chip die on mid-large screens, and refined mobile portrait/landscape HUD containment and safe-area insets.
- **Nearest-neighbor HiDPI rendering & live resize tracking:** Prioritized `image-rendering: pixelated` in CSS and added `ResizeObserver` plus fullscreen event listeners in `visualizer.js` so layout shifts and pseudo-fullscreen always keep canvas and Scene-DJ dimensions sharp and synchronized.

### 🎛️ Scene-DJ Engine Reliability & PAL Precision
- **Zero dropped beats at >50 Hz:** `StageManager` now latches `beatStarted` events and peak onset values across cached PAL ticks on 60/120/144 Hz monitors and delivers them cleanly once on the next rendered 50 Hz PAL frame.
- **Smoother layer & climax choreography:** `SetlistManager` sequences opaque `background` and `floor` swaps so outgoing scenes fade out completely before new scenes enter, defers swaps during `climax` and `afterglow`, enforces `maxInstances` consistently, and defers `TrackPresenter` activation until audio playback starts.
- **Consistent tension, Atari novelty & zero-idle overhead:** `TrackMonitor` uses clamped `[0, 1]` exponential smoothing, `TensionManager` normalizes momentum across frame rates and detects Atari YM2149 tone/volume novelty, and `StageManager` skips rendering and offscreen allocation for `idle` DSEs.

### ⚡ Amiga OCS DSE Polish
- **`AmigaRetroSunset`:** Replaces the unmasked horizon beam and widening road-like water reflection with `1 px` `1/z` perspective Copper water scanlines, Copper-wobbled shoreline mountain reflections, and split crimson/gold wave ribbons with scattered specular glints. Closes mountain valley seams, fixes even-radius climax sun slit parity, and preserves blue-hour tones during `stopping`.
- **`AmigaBoingBall`:** Removes the rectangular red/gold floor reflection box in favor of the pure dithered checkerboard shadow, adds hard `[0..w, 0..h]` polygon/silhouette clipping, hardens `starting`/`stopping` transition timing, and cools afterglow highlights across a crisp 12-step OCS ramp.
- **`KefrensCheckerboard`:** Replaces the sunset-aligned reflection cone with background-independent roaming Copper disco hotspots (single in calm states, dual crossing spots in `buildup`/`climax`) tinted by each active Copper palette, preserves afterglow cooling during `stopping`, fixes Bayer frontier stepping, and locks micro-glints to scrolling tiles.

## 🧪 Verification
Two new headless Node test suites (`test/dj-engine-lifecycle.test.mjs` and `test/scene-dj-integration.test.mjs`) alongside expanded beat-routing and smoothing tests cover the Scene-DJ engine contracts (`node --test`), while live browser playback verifies fullscreen, resize, and multi-layer stage behavior before publishing this release out of draft.
