# 🕹️ Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.0

### The DSE Overhaul: Authentic Demoscene Polish Across Amiga, C64 & Atari ST

Version 1.6.0 gives twelve classic system-specific **Demo-Scene-Elements (DSEs)** a ground-up bare-metal overhaul. Every updated background, floor, and foreground effect now runs deterministically on the exact 50 Hz PAL retro-buffer contract—free of alpha blending, browser anti-aliasing, DOM sprite overhead, or viewport cropping—while reacting cleanly to **beat strength**, **exact beat triggers**, **musical tension**, and **live chip channels**.

## ✨ Highlights

### ⚡ Amiga 12-Bit OCS/ECS Overhaul
- **`KefrensCheckerboard`:** Now cycles across four Copper palettes plus a cool *Moonlit Indigo* afterglow, switches scanline tile patterns (Classic, Beveled Slabs, Cyber Grid, Racing Check), bends into Copper line-scroll S-curves during buildups and climaxes, and fires traveling Z-shockwaves aligned seamlessly with `RetroSunset`.
- **`PaulaSiliconBg`:** Rebuilt purely with integer `fillRect` geometry—featuring sky-clipped octant Moiré interference rings, non-overlapping L-R-R-L DMA hardware towers, and illuminated 3D silicon bus traces driven by all four Paula channels.
- **`AmigaRetroSunset`:** Gains a second parallax cloud layer, aspect-stable tiled mountain ridges with beat-lit rim lines, climax solar raster slits, viewport-safe seagulls and leaping fish, and a smooth blue-hour dusk afterglow.

### 🟪 Commodore 64 VIC-II Overhaul
- **`ChunkyPlasma`:** Upgrades chunky 8×8 blocks with animated PETSCII sub-block dithering across four Colodore ramps and four plasma formations (Dual-Sine, Twin-Orb, Manhattan Diamond, Hyper-Vortex), modulated by all three SID voices.
- **`C64RetroSunset`:** Preserves its clean background serenity while adding ordered sky-band dither seams, time-based inner-edge sun dithering, climax sun slits, traveling `$D016` water sparkle waves, and a twilight afterglow.
- **`SidSiliconBg`:** Adapts automatically between side-by-side landscape and stacked portrait chip layouts, synthesizes fallback waveforms smoothly when registers are absent, and replaces whiteout flashes with beat-reactive bus and VCF highlights.
- **`C64VectorStar`:** Keeps its iconic 6510 mechanical 3-tick rotation stutter on a PAL-safe time base while adding four Y-Raster-Interrupt palettes, 2-pass Z-depth rendering, opaque 6510 wireframe ghost trails, and safe-area Lissajous paths.

### 🟩 Atari ST 9-Bit Shifter Overhaul
- **`AtariDotTorus`:** Restores smooth 3-axis 50 Hz PAL rotation (fixing Z-axis stagnation), adds depth-cued `1×1` / `2×2` dot passes, four Shifter palettes, 3-lobe/spiral knot morphs, opaque ghost trails, and a *Midnight Steel* afterglow.
- **`AtariBobs`:** Renders 4-ring Metal-Bobs via precomputed scanline circle LUTs without DOM canvases, fixes single-quadrant clamping into symmetric 4-way kaleidoscope mirroring, and adds 3D chain-wave bob sizing.
- **`AtariRetroSunset`:** Repairs path colors, line clipping, and shrine/Torii framing across all displays, sculpting curved tapered Bonsai boughs tucked beneath sunlit foliage cushions with drifting beat-triggered petals and a Zen moonlit afterglow.
- **`WireframeMorph`:** Fits cleanly inside any viewport from portrait to ultrawide while adding four Shifter themes, 2-pass Z-depth edges, opaque ghost trails, and an inner-cube dual-shell crystal mode.
- **`YmSiliconBg`:** Adapts cleanly between landscape columns and portrait rows, replacing blocking `getImageData()`/`putImageData()` tears and white strobes with Log-DAC Copper shockwaves, deterministic LFSR noise rain, and zero-readback Timer-B Sync-Buzzer scanline shifts.

## 📱 Responsive & Musical Precision
Whether you listen on a desktop 16:9 monitor, a 2560×1080 ultrawide screen, or rotate a smartphone between portrait and landscape mid-track, all twelve effects rescale their projections seamlessly on the same live instance—keeping simulation phases, rotations, and beat counters intact without jumps or empty margins.

## 🎧 What to try
Play a rhythmic track on each system—notice how **soft, medium, and hard beats** scale physical kicks and Copper pulses differently, how **buildup** transitions smoothly into **climax** formations, and how **afterglow** cools the entire visual stage down gracefully before fade-out.
