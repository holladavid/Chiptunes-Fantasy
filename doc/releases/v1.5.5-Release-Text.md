# GitHub Release Description

**Von Nerds für Nerds:** v1.5.5 brings four carefully documented Amiga conversions from *The Great Giana Sisters* to the music disk and turns the classic bottom scroller into an adaptive source of song, replay, and museum information.

The curated collection now contains **209 directly playable tracks**: **73 Amiga**, **50 C64**, and **86 Atari ST** titles. All previous playlist entries remain available. The v1.5.4 Amiga Exact, C64, and Atari foundations are carried forward without an audio-engine change in this release.

No MP3s or prerecorded audio are used. Historical binary music formats are parsed and synthesized in real time.

## Highlights

- **Four Giana Amiga conversions:** Title Screen, Get Ready!, In-Game 1 – Ground, and In-Game 2 – Cave, each with curated titles, museum research, source links, and hash-bound provenance.
- **Honest format history:** Chris Hülsbeck's compositions, Thomas Lopatic's Amiga music and Beathoven Synthesizer replay, and the later ProTracker conversion files are explicitly distinguished.
- **Adaptive TRACK channel:** curated song title, available composer credit, active chip system, and current SID/HIPC subsong.
- **Adaptive TECH channel:** real module, SID, or YM metadata rather than generic loader slogans.
- **Adaptive STORY channel:** concise background text derived from the selected song's museum research.
- **Restrained motion and direct control:** lower-amplitude sine movement, seamless wrapping, hover hold, HOLD/RUN, keyboard operation, touch-safe controls, and reduced-motion support.
- **Verified playback:** 73/73 Amiga tracks pass offline acceptance, and every Giana conversion also passes real desktop- and mobile-viewport Chromium playback.

## The Great Giana Sisters on Amiga

A dedicated **Giana Sisters** folder now contains four individually playable conversions:

| Track | Local file | Supplied MOD structure |
|---|---|---|
| Title Screen | `title-screen.mod` | 25 orders, 23 patterns, 8 samples |
| Get Ready! | `get-ready.mod` | 1 order, 1 pattern, 8 samples |
| In-Game 1 – Ground | `ingame-1-ground.mod` | 12 orders, 12 patterns, 8 samples |
| In-Game 2 – Cave | `ingame-2-cave.mod` | 17 orders, 13 patterns, 8 samples |

All four use the existing four-channel MOD parser and Paula playback path. Their shared 64,922-byte sample bank remains intact. The Cave order list reuses four earlier patterns, so 17 orders with only 13 stored patterns is expected—not a parser defect.

The museum cards credit **Thomas Lopatic** for the 1988 Amiga music and **Chris Hülsbeck** for the underlying Giana compositions. They also explain that Lopatic used his **Beathoven Synthesizer** on Amiga. The supplied MODs are later conversions, not original BSS modules, and the files contain no verified conversion-author credit.

Only these four verified browser-compatible conversions are included. **Options** and **High Scores** are not replaced with unsuitable files; C64 SIDs, Atari YM dumps, BRSTMs, MP3s, and other prerecorded substitutes are not presented as Amiga recordings.

## A scroller that knows what is playing

The bottom strip retains its demoscene identity but now has three useful channels:

- **TRACK:** the real curated title instead of `LOAD SID`, `LOAD PROTRACKER`, or a filename, followed by available credits and subsong information.
- **TECH:** Paula/MOD details, SID load/play addresses and subsong counts, or YM player rate, clock, and Digidrum data from the current file.
- **STORY:** a short, track-specific excerpt from the museum's historical research.

The text starts visibly after a song or channel change, moves at an elapsed-time-based speed, and wraps seamlessly. The sine displacement is deliberately subtle. Hovering holds the text; **HOLD/RUN** provides an explicit pause control. Native buttons support keyboard and touch input, and reduced-motion preferences stop automatic movement until the listener opts in.

On mobile, the strip is bounded at **108px plus the device safe area**, preventing uncontrolled canvas growth while retaining 44px touch targets.

## Suggested listening checks

1. Open **AMIGA → giana-sisters → Title Screen** and switch between **TRACK**, **TECH**, and **STORY**.
2. Compare **Get Ready!** with the two longer in-game conversions and check how each title updates immediately.
3. Select a HIPC track with multiple songs: the scroller follows the selected subsong without calling the format ProTracker.
4. Try a C64 RSID/PSID entry and an Atari YM track to see their format-specific technical lines.
5. Use **HOLD** to inspect a long line, then **RUN** to continue; with reduced motion enabled, automatic movement starts only after explicit opt-in.

## Validation

- **Full suite:** **367 tests — 359 passed, 7 skipped, 1 known pre-existing failure**. The suite is not reported as completely green.
- **Focused feature/curation checks:** **23/23 passed** for scroller content, wrapping, Giana metadata and provenance, browser evidence, and tracklist navigation.
- **Amiga curated corpus:** **73/73 passed** audible, finite, deterministic, block-size-independent, and restart-identical offline acceptance. No silent A500 title was found.
- **Giana browser acceptance:** all four conversions produced audible, finite output through the production UI on desktop and mobile viewports, with a running `AudioContext` and no console, page, request, dialog, or HTTP errors.
- **Scroller browser acceptance:** channel changes, per-track text updates, hover hold, HOLD/RUN, reduced-motion handling, explicit opt-in, source links, and mobile touch targets passed.
- **Responsive layout:** checks at **375, 768, 1024, and 1440px** found no horizontal document overflow; the scroller remains within its bounded height.

The single full-suite failure remains `SID synthetic corpus matches the reviewed Phase 3 raw/trace/WAV hashes`. For `noise-adsr`, the actual hash remains `d26ae30f7221b719eb6dfdebb5b77b7ed5f841662c28ab7d9943a5e522d93ff0`; the reviewed baseline was not overwritten.

## Known limitations

- The supplied Giana MODs are conversions. This release does not introduce a Beathoven Synthesizer/BSS replayer.
- No suitable verified Amiga conversions were selected for **Options** or **High Scores**.
- Scroller stories are derived from existing curated research; they are not generated historical claims and do not use an external AI service.
- The pre-existing SID synthetic-corpus mismatch remains open, and no failing baseline was replaced to hide it.
- Offline measurements and headless Chromium viewports do not prove physical-device latency, underrun freedom, or listening quality.
- Amiga A500 filter values remain documented software approximations, not measured mainboard calibration.
- Atari Exact remains a fixed-2-MHz register-dump player, and C64/SID random-access seeking remains unavailable.
- Public availability in Modland or HVSC does not imply public domain, redistribution permission, or commercial-use rights. Rights remain with their respective holders.

## Run locally

Serve the source tree over HTTP(S); ES modules and `AudioWorklet` loading are not intended for `file://` URLs.

```bash
npx serve .
# or
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Open the shown local URL, enter through the boot screen, and select **AMIGA**, **COMMODORE 64**, or **ATARI ST**.
