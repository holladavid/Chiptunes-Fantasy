# 💾 Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.6

### Interactive CRT & Scope Calibration Deck, Linear-Light WebGL CRT Pipeline & 100 Hz-Capped Oscilloscope

Version 1.6.6 turns the display stage into a **live-tunable CRT & Oscilloscope Calibration Laboratory**. Dial in authentic 15 kHz RGB, Commodore 1702 Composite, Trinitron Aperture Grille, or Delta-dot phosphor monitors alongside full front-panel oscilloscope controls—complete with persistent `localStorage` calibration, one-key **A/B factory comparison**, a **linear-light WebGL CRT shader overhaul**, and a **framerate-independent, 100 Hz-capped oscilloscope engine**.

---

## ✨ Highlights

### 🎛️ Interactive CRT & Scope Calibration Deck (`[ CRT / SCOPE ]`, Shortcut `[C]`)
- **Live Calibration & Persistent Storage:** Open the calibration rack via `[ CRT / SCOPE ]` or pressing **`C`**. All CRT and oscilloscope adjustments persist automatically in `localStorage` (`chiptunes-fantasy.display-settings.v1`), with double-click per-slider reset and global `[ RESET ]`.
- **6 Hardware Monitor Profiles:** Switch between **FACTORY**, **AUTO SYS** (automatically selects `1702 COMP` for C64, `1084S RGB` for Amiga, and `TRINITRON` for Atari ST while retaining your GPU quality setting), **1084S RGB**, **1702 COMP**, **TRINITRON**, and **DELTA**.
- **Instant A/B Live Comparison (`[B]`):** Toggle **A/B** (`ORIG`) at any time to compare your custom calibration directly against factory defaults on the live stage without losing your settings; tweaking any slider exits comparison mode automatically.
- **Adaptive Responsive Layouts:**
  - **Desktop / Widescreen:** 2-column rack below the playback deck (expanding to 3-column CRT / 2-column Scope slider grids on `≥ 1500px` displays).
  - **Fullscreen Stage:** Right-docked floating rack (`min(440px, 42vw)`) mounted directly inside `#visual-zone`.
  - **Mobile Portrait (`≤ 800px`):** Pins the live stage at the top with a 44 px touch-friendly calibration sheet below.
  - **Mobile Landscape (`≤ 1024×580`):** Live **50/50 side-by-side split** with the stage on the left and controls on the right.

### 📺 Linear-Light WebGL CRT Shadow Mask Overhaul (`crt-shadow-mask.js`)
- **Linear-Light Pipeline (Gamma 2.0):** Samples, filters, mixes halation, and calculates spot blooming in linear light (`c * c` → `sqrt` output) on a seam-free fullscreen triangle with `highp` vertex coordinates (`>1000 px` mobile-GPU safe).
- **Analytical Pixel-Snapped Phosphor Geometry:** Choose between **Slot**, **Delta** (hexagonal triad dots), and **Aperture Grille** (Trinitron) masks. Exact 1-px box-filtered coverage snaps cleanly to integer device-pixel triads for moiré-free sharpness at any DPR without darkening the image.
- **Two-Pass Low-Res Halation & Tube Optics:** In **`HIGH`** quality mode, a 4:1 linear box-filter FBO pass generates smooth 5-tap glass halation free of echo contours, paired with tunable **Mask Pitch** (`2–8 px`), **Mask Depth** (`0–130%`), **Scanlines** (`0–100%`, `1X`/`2X` rows), **Beam Blur** (`0–1.00 px`), **R/B Convergence Drift** (`0–4.0 px`), **Tube Curvature** (`FLAT`–`5.6%`) with rounded bezel SDF, **Vignette** (`0–80%`), **Contrast** (`0.75x–1.75x`), ambient glass sheen, and Jimenez noise dithering (`±0.5 LSB`).
- **Graceful Quality & CSS Fallbacks:** Switch between **`LOW`**, **`MED`**, and **`HIGH`** GPU tiers at runtime, with automatic FBO fallback to `MED` and CSS fallback if hardware WebGL or `highp` fragment precision is unavailable.

### 📈 Configurable, Framerate-Independent Phosphor Oscilloscope (`oscilloscope.js`)
- **100 Hz Simulation Cap & Display-Rate Independence:** Decouples simulation stepping (`MIN_STEP_SECONDS = 0.01`) from frame composition and scales memory persistence proportionally to step fade rates, delivering identical phosphor glow and decay on 60 Hz, 120 Hz, 144 Hz, and 240 Hz monitors at reduced cost.
- **Level-Normalized Trigger & Optimized Layer Rendering:** Normalizes Schmitt trigger slope and `-MSE` correlation to peak and signal power so quiet passages lock cleanly; draws trace layers sequentially instead of column-by-column (halving canvas style switches); introduces a `dwellLut` lookup table; centers bloom vertically on trace segments; and extends CSS color parsing (`#hex`, `rgb()`, `rgba()`, named/HSL colors).
- **Front-Panel Scope Controls:** Fine-tune **V/DIV Gain** (`0.50x–2.50x`), **Timebase** (`8–36 ms`), **Persistence** (`0–100%`), **Phosphor Glow** (`0–100%`), **Graticule** (`OFF`–`100%`), **Beam FX** (`SPARKS` / `SPOT` / `OFF`), **Trigger Mode** (`AUTO` / `FREE`), and **Phosphor Tint** (`SYS` / `P31` green / `P11` cyan / `AMBER`).
