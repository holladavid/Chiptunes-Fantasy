# GitHub Release Description

**Von Nerds für Nerds:** v1.5.5 is a targeted mobile hotfix that keeps the Chiptunes Fantasy visualization usable on iPhones, removes permanently visible fullscreen chrome, and returns valuable screen space to the song library.

The runtime fix is confined to responsive CSS. No SID, Paula, YM, parser, replay, audio-engine, or Scene-DJ/DSE behavior was changed. The curated collection remains at **209 directly playable tracks**: **73 Amiga**, **50 C64**, and **86 Atari ST** titles.

## Highlights

- **Windowed overlay auto-hide:** the Now Playing / **BUS ACTIVE** card now disappears after the existing three-second inactivity period instead of permanently covering the visualization.
- **iPhone safe-area repair:** the windowed stage starts below the top sensor-housing inset, while fullscreen controls respect device cutouts. The logo and Lab Mode remain reachable.
- **Completely unobstructed fullscreen:** all stage chrome hides after inactivity, including the **EXIT** button. Touching or moving within the stage brings the controls back.
- **Much smaller mobile control area:** four transport buttons occupy one row, progress and volume share the next, and computer/core selection uses tighter spacing.
- **More room for music:** at the tested 393 px viewport width, the playback bar measures **95 px instead of 255 px** high—a reduction of approximately **63%**—without removing any control.

## What changed

### Windowed visualization

The Now Playing card follows the same inactivity behavior in windowed mode that was already used for fullscreen telemetry. It fades away after three seconds and reappears on interaction. The visualization no longer begins underneath the iPhone's top safe area, so the Scene-DJ logo toggle and Lab Mode are not trapped beneath the notch.

### Fullscreen inactivity behavior

The special rule that kept the fullscreen exit control permanently visible has been removed. The fullscreen bar, playback controls, stage status, Now Playing card, collapsed hardware HUD, and logo now hide together. Both native fullscreen selectors and the iOS pseudo-fullscreen fallback are covered.

The exit action has not been removed: interact with the stage to reveal the controls, then use **EXIT** as before.

### Compact mobile controls

The mobile playback bar now uses two compact rows:

1. Previous, Play/Pause, Next, and Pure Audio controls.
2. Progress/time controls beside the volume control.

C64, Amiga, and Atari typography retain their respective themes. System tabs and the core selector remain available with reduced vertical spacing. Desktop layout and all playback logic are unchanged.

## Suggested device checks

1. In windowed mode, start a track and leave the stage untouched for three seconds; the **BUS ACTIVE** card should disappear.
2. On a notched iPhone, confirm that the visualization begins below the sensor housing and that both the logo and **Lab Mode** can be operated.
3. Enter fullscreen and wait three seconds; no controls or information cards should remain visible.
4. Touch the stage to wake the controls, then leave fullscreen with **EXIT**.
5. Check transport, progress, volume, system tabs, core selection, and song navigation in portrait orientation.

## Validation

- **iPhone-sized Chromium viewport:** 393 × 659 px, covering windowed mode and the iOS-style pseudo-fullscreen fallback.
- **Automatic hiding:** fullscreen bar, exit control through its parent, Now Playing card, logo, and collapsed HUD reached zero opacity and disabled pointer interaction after inactivity.
- **Wake-up and exit:** stage interaction restored the controls; exiting pseudo-fullscreen returned the visualization to its original position in `demo-container`.
- **Safe-area simulation:** a 47 px top inset placed the visualization at `y=47`; Lab Mode was reachable at `y=66` and opened successfully.
- **Compact sizing:** the playback bar measured 95 px, down from 255 px before the hotfix.
- **Narrow-screen check:** 320 × 568 px layouts showed no horizontal document overflow across C64, Amiga, and Atari control configurations.

The full automated test suite was not rerun for this CSS-only hotfix. Headless Chromium viewports and simulated safe-area values are not a substitute for a final check on a physical iPhone.

## Scope and known limitations

- No audio, emulation, parser, playlist, museum, Scene-DJ, or DSE code was changed.
- No new tracks or features were added; v1.5.5 content and playlist paths remain intact.
- Native iOS fullscreen behavior can vary by browser and iOS version; the existing pseudo-fullscreen fallback remains supported.
- The known limitations documented for v1.5.5, including the pre-existing SID synthetic-corpus mismatch and unavailable C64/SID random-access seeking, are outside this hotfix's scope.
- The planned per-DSE historical-fidelity optimization is not part of this release.

## Upgrade notes

Replace `css/style.css` with the v1.5.6 version. No data migration, dependency update, or audio-cache rebuild is required. A normal network-first load refreshes the stylesheet; if an older installed PWA shell retains stale assets, reload after its offline cache is updated or cleared.
