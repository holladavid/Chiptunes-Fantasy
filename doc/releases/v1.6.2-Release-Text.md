# 🕹️ Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.2

### Atari ST Director's Cut Audio, Winter Gothic Castle & IK+ Sunset Choreography

Version 1.6.2 delivers a major **Atari ST audio and visual showcase** alongside **fullscreen inactivity UI polish** and contributor governance.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🎛️ Atari ST YM2149F "Blade Runner" Core v2.5 (Director's Cut)
- **Authentic CS-80 voice architecture:** Replaces `MoogFilter` with Padé-[3/2] **Zero-Delay Feedback SVFs (`ZDFFilter`)** featuring soft bandpass saturation, dual-rank detuned PWM/sawtooth VCOs with Nyquist roll-off, ~9 ms legato glide (tracking fast arpeggios and tracker slides 1:1), 12 dB HPF + 12 dB resonant LPF, and **post-VCF pure Sine addition**.
- **32-step hardware envelope & YM6 MFP-timer FX:** Adds 32-step `YM_DAC` envelope interpolation, clean silencing of expired one-shot envelopes, PolyBLEP sawtooth buzzer anti-aliasing, and MFP-clocked decoding for **YM6 Digidrum sample rates (2.5–32 kHz)**, **Atari SID-Voice**, and **TAO Sync-Buzzer** effects with a 1.5 ms anti-click Digidrum crossfade.
- **Sample-rate-independent stereo space pipeline:** Adds polynomial equal-power panning, a mono-compatible **True-Stereo Dimension-D BBD Chorus**, flutter-modulated **Tape Multi-Tap Delay**, a **19 ms pre-delay + 2× Allpass + 4-channel quadrature-modulated Lexicon 224 FDN Concert Hall Reverb**, asymmetric tape saturation, and signed block-peak visual telemetry.

### 🏰 Atari ST DSE Upgrades
- **`AtariEnchantedCastle`:** Reimagined in strict 16-color, 9-bit Shifter pixel art as a snow-clad Carpathian/Dolomite Vampire Fortress (*Tanz der Vampire* × Caspar David Friedrich) honoring the `0.55` floor-DSE horizon. Replaces DOM-dependent fades and `globalAlpha` with native **4×4 Bayer ordered-dither transitions**, pre-baked Dolomite couloir, weathered masonry, and spruce-tuft LUTs, cylindrical Bayer-shaded towers, an 80-flake 3-layer winter snowfall, valley spruce groves, snow-fence/hollow-way/bridge and hunter silhouettes, YM channel (`smooth[0..2]`) lighting/motion, and 2.5 s `afterglow` Blood-Moon cooling.
- **`AtariRetroSunset` + `ik-fighter-sprites.js`:** Introduces a 100% DOM-free 9-bit Shifter sprite engine compiling 29 hand-crafted IK+ martial-arts keyframes into zero-allocation RLE spans. Each fade-in rolls between **`karateka`** (white Gi & red Hachimaki) and **`kaempferin`** (sleeveless Gi, crimson wraps/obi, indigo trousers & animated ponytail), executing 50 Hz PAL-aligned strikes, beat canceling, parabolic jumps, climax sparks, opening/closing bows, and Zen katas behind foreground drifting petals.

### 🖥️ Fullscreen Inactivity UI & Governance
- **Cleaner fullscreen auto-hide:** Fades `#brand-logo` together with `#fs-ui-bar` and `.now-playing` during fullscreen inactivity, prevents retained button focus from pinning `#fs-ui-bar` open, keeps `#brand-logo` visible in windowed mobile landscape while auto-hiding in fullscreen, evaluates fullscreen state at timeout execution, wakes overlays on `touchmove` and `fullscreenchange`, and updates the boot header to `V1.6.2`.
- **Governance:** Adds a Contributor Covenant v2.0 `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

## Scope
- Exact/reference emulation cores (`SID`, `Paula`, `YM2149 Exact`), `dseRegistry` assignments, and the 214-track curated library remain unchanged.
- Zero external runtime dependencies added.
