## GitHub Release Description

# 💾 Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.7

### 18 New System-Authentic Demo-Scene Elements (6 per Hardware Pillar), Dedicated C64 & Atari ST Floor Layers & Shared DOM-Free Retro Rendering Kit

Version 1.6.7 delivers the largest visual stage expansion in Chiptunes Fantasy history: **18 brand-new, historically faithful Demo-Scene Elements (DSEs)**—**6 for Commodore 64**, **6 for Amiga OCS/ECS**, and **6 for Atari ST**—spanning `background`, `floor`, and `foreground`. Both **C64** and **Atari ST** gain dedicated perspective **`floor` layers** for the first time, **Amiga** expands its ground arsenal with 3D voxel mountain raycasting and dual-playfield parallax terrain, and every effect runs natively in Node/browser via a **DOM-free, zero-allocation retro graphics kit** synchronized to musical beats, tension curves, and exact soundchip channels.

## ✨ Highlights

### 🟦 Commodore 64 — VIC-II & 3-Voice SID (`js/visuals/dse/c64/*`)
All 6 effects strictly enforce the 16-color `C64_PALETTE`, scanline/run-merged integer drawing, and 3-channel SID (`smooth[0..2]`) reactivity:
- **`C64RasterTunnel` (`background`):** Nested aspect-aware intro raster tunnel anchored to `(width/2, horizon - 1)` so active floors serve as the tunnel floor, featuring a 32-ring SID waterfall (`lv[]` updated solely on far-ring entry), `climax` split-shading, a 6-slot pulse ring pool, and depth fog.
- **`C64FireWall` (`background`):** 50 Hz cellular heat simulation (`2–4 px` chunky cells aligned flush down to `horizon` with `Nearest-Neighbor` resize preservation), 3 cosine-interpolated SID flame zones, 2×2 step dithering, ember bed, a 24-spark `Int16Array` pool, and "cold blue fire" in `afterglow`.
- **`C64RasterRoad` (`floor`):** Classic Outrun-style scanline perspective road (`y >= horizon`) with 4 wipeable terrain families (`Grass`, `Desert`, `Ice`, `Night`), quadratic road curves, anti-moiré horizon haze dithering, 3 hysteresis-gated SID lanes, Z-sorted roadside pylons & hard-beat gates (`vEff <= 1.7`), and `climax` speed streaks.
- **`C64DanceFloor` (`floor`):** 61×9 perspective Color-RAM tile matrix (`y >= horizon`) running a 50 Hz 4-pattern engine (*Diagonal Chase*, *SID Waterfall*, *3-Slot Ripple*, *Searchlight*), 3 SID sparkle zones, `climax` rainbow rows, and an automatic 2 Hz beatless clock fallback.
- **`C64SineScroller` (`foreground`):** Two-pass outlined (`1 px` black) DYCP sine scroller using the shared `5×7` bitmap font with Raster-IRQ color bands, hysteresis-gated grey echo line, automatic `wide`/`tall` orientation preservation across rotations, and non-black scroll-in/scroll-out transitions.
- **`C64BorderRasters` (`foreground`):** VIC-II `$D020` open-border raster bars with 3 SID bar groups, `1 px` black inner framing, `climax` 16-px diagonal shear stripes, and an 8-sprite `7×7` star pool orbiting the border centerline.

### 🟥 Amiga OCS/ECS — Copper, Blitter & 4-Channel Paula (`js/visuals/dse/amiga/*`)
All 6 effects use the new immutable 12-bit OCS palette kit (`amiga-kit.js`, `<= 32` colors per DSE, `PP6` luminance-balanced ping-pong marches, and `PAULA_LRRL = [0, 3, 1, 2]` matching `paula-silicon-bg.js`):
- **`AmigaRotoZoomer` (`background`):** 4×4-chunky-style rotozoomer (`<= 6000` cells with run-merging) across 4 precomputed `64×64` procedural textures (*Diamonds*, *Beveled Checker*, *Bricks*, *Interference*), tension-driven 1×/2×/4× kaleidoscope folding, screen-space Paula brightness zones, Copper vignette, and beat Copper flash bands.
- **`AmigaTechTech` (`background`):** Copper `BPLCON1` + Blitter horizontal scanline-shift curtain across 4 periodic patterns (*Checkerboard*, *Curtain*, *Diamonds*, *Bricks*), 3-harmonic wave displacement (`|dx| <= 0.2 * minDim`), 4 vertical Paula amplitude zones, and a sweeping 6-row hard-beat shockwave.
- **`AmigaVoxelHills` (`floor`):** Comanche-style `128×128` wrapping periodic-noise height-map raycaster with pre-shaded 2D slopes, winding canyon flight path, 3D mountain peaks rising past `horizon` (up to `14%` sky ceiling with `1 px` black crest outlines and transparent sky gaps), 4 Paula waterfall mountain ridges, traveling Z-contour waves, and adaptive frame-time protection.
- **`AmigaParallaxGround` (`floor`):** *Shadow of the Beast*-inspired 8-band Dual-Playfield Copper parallax ground (`y >= horizon`) with 4 cascading-wipe terrain themes (*Meadow*, *Rock*, *Desert*, *Snow*), Paula-driven foliage height equalizer, `8×10` pixel-art props (6-slot pool), and a 16-particle dust system under gravity.
- **`AmigaTwister` (`foreground`):** Scanline rasterized 4-sided Amiga twister scaling dynamically from 1 to 3 phase-offset bars on widescreen/ultrawide, blending smoothly across 4 twist profiles (*Linear*, *Sawtooth*, *Zigzag Helix*, *Pulsing*) with each face permanently bound to Paula channels `0..3`.
- **`AmigaVectorBalls` (`foreground`):** Pre-shaded `Int16Array` Blitter-bob sphere templates (`r = 2..14`) Z-sorted back-to-front across 4 morphing 3D formations (*Cube Grid*, *Double Helix*, *Fibonacci Sphere*, *Trefoil Knot*), index-activated `18 → 28 → 36` balls, counter-rotating ultrawide twin bodies, and `climax` ring-buffered ghost trails.

### 🟩 Atari ST — 9-Bit Shifter, Timer-B Rasters & 3-Voice YM2149 (`js/visuals/dse/atari/*`)
All 6 effects strictly enforce 9-bit Shifter RGB (`0..7 × 36`, max `#fcfcfc` via `atari-kit.js`), `<= 16` colors per scanline, and **Timer-B scanline-split wipes (`wipeY`)** that divide crossing primitives cleanly at the raster boundary:
- **`AtariMetaBlobs` (`background`):** Chunky metaball field (`<= 5000` cells) featuring 3 YM2149 voice blobs + up to 4 orbiting satellites across 4 morphing orbital shapes (*Ellipse*, *Figure-8*, *Cross*, *Longitudinal*), long-axis responsive anchoring, and `climax` `#fcfcfc` cores (`<= 14` colors/scanline).
- **`AtariJuliaCycler` (`background`):** Classic Atari ST Julia fractal (`<= 6000` cells) morphing across 4 constants (*Classic Spiral*, *Douady Rabbit*, *Siegel Disk*, *Dendrite*) with 3 radial YM2149 phase zones and a flicker-safe palette ring (`RING_HUE`, capped at `<= 6 steps/s`).
- **`AtariLaserGrid` (`floor`):** Perspective neon wireframe grid (`y >= horizon`) with vanishing-point drift, anti-moiré scanline haze, continuous perspective column spans, 50% scanline-dithered YM2149 lane glows, 4-slot pulse lines, decaying grid sine waves, and `climax` laser sweeps (`<= 6` colors/scanline).
- **`AtariPondRipples` (`floor`):** Perspective water plane (`y >= horizon`) with row-dithered depth shading, 24 `Xorshift32` glints, 3 YM2149 wave emitters, deterministic ambient droplets, 12-slot concentric ripple pool rendered via run-merged Midpoint ellipses, and 12 spray streaks (`<= 6` colors/scanline).
- **`AtariFlatShadedTrio` (`foreground`):** Classic Cuddly-Demos / Starglider-style flat-shaded 3D polyhedron trio cycling 6 unit-normalized meshes (*Cube*, *Octahedron*, *Tetrahedron*, *Triangular Prism*, *Pyramid*, *Hex Bipyramid*) with dual backface culling, Painter Z-sort, deduplicated `1 px` black edge pass, face explosion, and `0.40 s` geometric mesh swaps.
- **`AtariLineRibbons` (`foreground`):** Mystify-style Bresenham line ribbons across 3 YM2149 polygons (`4` vertices, `5` in `climax`), `25 Hz` 8-slot history ring buffer with instant `snapNow` on medium/hard beats, `(+1,+1)` black contrast shadow + double head, and proportional vertex & history remapping on resize (`<= 1 px` tolerance).

- **21 New Strict Unit Test Suites (`test/*.test.mjs`):** Added 18 per-DSE strict fake-canvas suites + 3 helper/kit suites enforcing integer/bounds clipping, exact 16-color / 12-bit / 9-bit hardware palettes, scanline color limits, `y >= horizon` floor guards, 300-frame determinism, graded soft/medium/hard beat responses, rotation continuity, and `fillRect` budgets.
