## GitHub Release Description

# 💾 Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.9

### Emulation & Timing Calibration Deck, WebGL CRT Optical & `GPU: OFF` Upgrades, Unobstructed Windowed Stage & LimitBar v4.0.0

Version 1.6.9 expands the Calibration Deck into a **3-column hardware rack** with the new **`EMULATION & TIMING`** category (selectable PAL VBL engine rates, adjustable Tension buildup speed, restored C64 SID silicon temperature control, and a live `LimitBar` toggle), adds a **`GPU: OFF`** shader bypass alongside key **WebGL CRT optical/alignment fixes**, brings **3-second overlay auto-hiding** into windowed mode so Oscilloscope, FFT, and DSEs can be enjoyed distraction-free, and ships a complete **v4.0.0 Demoscene Hardware Overhaul of `LimitBar`**.

---

## ✨ Highlights

### ⚙️ New `EMULATION & TIMING` Calibration Category (`display-setup-dialog.js`, `display-settings.js`, `scene-dj.js`)
- **DSE Engine Refresh Rates (`50` / `25` / `16.7` / `12.5 Hz`):** Select authentic PAL vertical-blank frame divisors (`1 / 2 / 3 / 4 VBLs`) in `StageManager` to faithfully recreate classic multi-frame 3D/fractal demo engine framerates—while preserving beat latching across cached frames and keeping audio, Oscilloscope, and FFT at full smoothness.
- **Tension Buildup Speed (`SLOW` / `NORM` / `FAST`):** Tune how fast `TensionManager` climbs toward `buildup` and `climax` (`0.5×`, `1.0×`, `2.0×` accumulation with calibrated hold scaling).
- **C64 SID Temperature Slider (`15 °C – 75 °C`):** Restored live thermal cutoff and OTA saturation calibration (`SET_TEMPERATURE`, `55 °C` default) synced to C64 `AudioWorklet` cores on boot, core switching, live slider changes, `A/B` compare, and resets.
- **`LimitBar` Live Toggle (`OFF` / `ON`):** Toggle the bottom tension overlay dynamically via `SetlistManager.setPermanentDseEnabled()` while keeping static `dseRegistry` defaults untouched and running alongside `TrackPresenter` oneshot banners.
- **3-Column Rack & Glyph-Safe Layout:** Expanded the desktop Calibration Deck (`>= 1360 px`) to a 3-column rack, fixed wide `Press Start 2P` button clipping (`AMBER`, `GRILLE`, `16.7`, `12.5`) with proportional flex sizing (`flex: 1 1 auto`), and stretched `PHOSPHOR` & `DSE RATE (HZ)` across full panel width.

### 📺 WebGL CRT Shadow Mask: `GPU: OFF` & Optical Fixes (`crt-shadow-mask.js`, `visualizer.js`)
- **`GPU: OFF` Mode:** Skips WebGL shader compilation, FBO/texture allocation, `resize()`, and per-frame `texSubImage2D` uploads while releasing active GPU resources (`[ SHADER OFF · CSS ]`), cleanly falling back to CSS `.scanlines` with presets/sliders preserved and `A/B` Factory comparison active.
- **`DELTA` Pitch & Scanline Alignment:** Switched `delta` triad quantization from `step = 6` to `step = 2` (`min 4 px`) so `MASK PITCH` works across `DPR = 1` monitors without Moiré, and aligned `delta` scanline peaks directly onto phosphor dot rows (`1X`) and dot pairs (`2X`).
- **Unimodal 3-Tap Beam Filter & Spot-Blooming:** Replaced the bimodal 2-tap horizontal beam filter in `MED`/`HIGH` with a center-weighted 3-tap Gaussian (up to `1.5 px`) to prevent double contours on HiDPI screens, added energy-preserving scanline spot-blooming, widened the halation glow prefilter to an overlapping tent, and compiled `maskType` via `#define MASK_TYPE` to remove per-pixel branching.

### 🪟 Unobstructed Windowed Stage & Touch Wake Guard (`css/style.css`, `js/app.js`)
- **Windowed Overlay Auto-Hide:** `#brand-logo` and `#fs-ui-bar` now fade out after `3 s` of inactivity in windowed mode alongside `#chip-hud.collapsed` and `#now-playing`, leaving the Oscilloscope, FFT, and DSEs completely clean.
- **Safe Interaction Guards:** Disabled `pointer-events` on seekable progress bars while hidden/waking, added a touch wake-up guard (`overlays-waking`) so the first tap reveals controls without firing hidden buttons underneath, and reset the idle timer on boot and system switches.

### 📊 `LimitBar` v4.0.0 — Demoscene Hardware Edition (`js/visuals/dse/universal/limit-bar.js`)
- Rebuilt with zero per-frame allocations, deterministic `hashU32` math, `dt`-based slide transitions, `warmup: 0` onset visibility, `beatStarted`-gated phase updates, dark contrast bevel framing, and strict bounds clipping across all viewports:
  - **C64:** VIC-II `$D020/$D021` scanline rasterbars, 3 physical SID voice LEDs (`smooth[0..2]`), and beveled Colodore fat-pixel segments.
  - **Amiga:** Precomputed 64-step 12-bit OCS Copperlist LUTs with **Extra Half-Brite (EHB)** bottom shading, **L-R-R-L** 4-channel Paula DMA pips (`smooth[0..3]`), stepped blitter sweeps, and `climax` end-cap orbiters.
  - **Atari ST:** Strict 9-bit Shifter palette (max `#fcfcfc`), Timer-B bezel split pulses, 3 YM2149 voice pips (`smooth[0..2]`), and clipped Bresenham tip & edge vector sparks.
- **Asset Hygiene:** Normalized `tracks/c64/games/Ocean_Loader_1.sid` to `ocean-loader-1.sid`.
