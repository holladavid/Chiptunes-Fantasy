# 💾 Chiptunes Fantasy v1.6.8

### Atari ST 9-Bit Shifter & Timer-B Raster Overhaul, True 3D Raytraced Vector Bobs, TrackPresenter v3.2.0 & Midpoint Circle Parity

Version 1.6.8 pushes **Atari ST** visual authenticity and 3D demo craftsmanship to a whole new tier across **9 Atari DSEs and sprite sheets**, upgrades **`TrackPresenter` (`v3.2.0`)** with rock-steady centered typography and an authentic 9-bit Timer-B raster banner, refines **`hardware-constraints.js`** with exact Midpoint circle fill/outline parity, and expands automated Node.js strict fake-canvas verification.

---

## ✨ Highlights

### 🟩 Atari ST — 9-Bit Shifter, Timer-B Rasters & 3D Craftsmanship (`js/visuals/dse/atari/*`)
All Atari ST visuals enforce 9-bit Shifter RGB (`0..7 × 36`, max `#fcfcfc`), `<= 16` colors per scanline per layer, zero alpha blending, and crisp Timer-B scanline-split transitions:
- **`AtariLaserGrid` (`floor`):** Upgraded with **4×16 Timer-B Raster-Interrupt themes** (*Cyber Outrun*, *Synth Sunset*, *Solar Copper*, *Toxic Aurora* + `BLU5` afterglow, `<= 6` colors/scanline), moving shimmer bands, smooth Bresenham 3D waved horizontal curves, gapless run-merged waved perspective rays, converging YM2149 lanes, `#fcfcfc` vanishing-point & pulse-core flashes, and twin-core `climax` laser sweeps.
- **`AtariPondRipples` (`floor`):** Adds a curated 4th *North-Sea Teal* palette (`SEA5`), 3-zone perspective water depth with liquid-streak dithering, an HBL scanline shimmer column with YM2149 A/C side wisps, continuous flicker-free twinkling across 24 glints, 3D trough+crest waves with `#fcfcfc` peak highlights, a transparent Bayer-4×4 floor fade, and `1 px` Timer-B split wipes.
- **`AtariFlatShadedTrio` (`foreground`):** Replaced primitive solids with **6 planar-normalized crystal polyhedra** (*Cuboctahedron*, *Rhombic Dodecahedron*, *Square Antiprism*, *5-Sided Crystal*, *Octahedron*, *Hex Bipyramid*), front-hemisphere wrap-Lambert shading, `1 px` specular rim highlights, solid `INK` inner crystal cores during face explosions, `climax` opaque silhouette trails, and cross-body depth sorting.
- **`AtariBobs` (`lissajous-bobs.js`, `foreground`):** Rebuilt into a true **3D Lissajous & Knot Bob engine** across 6 formations (*Lissajous*, *Halo*, *Helix*, *Trefoil*, *Ribbon*, *Galaxy*) with Zero-GC Painter Z-sorting, precomputed **3D raytraced sphere scanline-run LUTs** (`r = 1..10`, zero `Math.sqrt` per frame), opaque `climax` echo trails, graded 3-tier traveling chain waves, cascading Midnight-Chrome afterglow, and `1.5 s` transitions.
- **`AtariLineRibbons` (`foreground`):** Expanded to a 16-slot history ring buffer with stride-2 airy trail fanning (`3 → 2 → 1` stages), cohesive anti-collapse rotation geometry, symmetrical `INK` double-head contrast shadows, corner jewels, and `#fcfcfc` hard-beat flashes.
- **`AtariRetroSunset` (`background`) & `ik-fighter-sprites.js`:** Upgraded to 10-stop Timer-B raster skies & 5-stop lake reflections, twinkling stars, 3 asymmetric hand-pixeled dusk cloud silhouettes, dual-layer twilight mountain valleys, *Toro* stone lanterns, a curved *Myojin* Torii gate with Kanji plaque, a gnarled *Moyogi* S-curve Pine Bonsai with 5 cloud tiers, and an 18-slot falling & courtyard-settling leaf pool—alongside refined kick anatomy across 7 frames in `ik-fighter-sprites.js`.
- **`WireframeMorph` (`foreground`):** Adds a counter-rotating **4D Hyper-Core**, Painter Z-sorting across up to 48 edges, theme-matched 3-step opaque echo trails, `1 px` `INK` drop shadows, `1 px` parallel bevel edges, Atari ST vertex star crosshairs, and a Lissajous figure-8 roaming trajectory.
- **`YmSiliconBg` (`background`):** Enforces a strict `0.55 * h` horizon split (DIP-40 YM2149F IC die, copper side rails with traveling bus electrons, 16-bit LFSR/envelope header & 3 oscillator chambers above; Log-DAC staircase & copper bus lanes below), 15-color 9-bit silicon palette, per-voice YM2149F waveforms with 10-segment LED peak meters & optional `metrics.regs` frequency/envelope decoding, `1.5 s` Bayer fades, and Timer-B Sync-Buzzer scanline tears.

### 🏷️ TrackPresenter v3.2.0 & Stage Timing (`track-presenter.js`, `registry.js`)
- **Centered Typography & 4.0 s Display Window:** Extended `TrackPresenter` duration from `3.0 s` to `4.0 s` and locked title/subtitle alignment cleanly centered across C64, Amiga, and Atari ST (scrolling only when wider than the frame).
- **Atari ST 9-Bit Timer-B Banner:** Redesigned with 4 demoscene palettes (*Goldrunner Emerald/Amber*, *ST Shifter Cobalt/Cyan*, *Union Crimson/Copper*, *Carebears Amethyst/Chrome*) + afterglow, 50 Hz PAL CRT shutter unfold, counter-moving rail glints, stepped 16-bit corner brackets & rivets, Copper-split dividers, and symmetrical 3-channel YM2149 raster wings.

### 🔧 Retro Rendering Primitives, Architecture Spec & Automated Tests
- **Midpoint Circle Parity & Hardening (`hardware-constraints.js`):** Replaced `Math.sqrt` circles in `fillAliasedCircle` with an exact odd-span integer Midpoint rasterizer matching `drawAliasedCircleOutline` 1:1 (deduplicated octant plots, optional `clipRect`, plus `*Clipped` aliases), hardened all quantization/line/polygon helpers against `NaN`/`Infinity`, and optimized `transparentDither` stepping.
- **DSE Hardware Rules (`doc/concepts/dse_development_rules.md`):** Documented authentic chip-specific raster techniques (*Hires/Multicolor/FLI*, *EHB/HAM6/Mid-Scanline Copper*, *Timer-B/Spectrum-512*), *Shade-Bobs*, strict `getImageData()` prohibition, and preallocated chunky/C2P `putImageData()` buffer rules.
- **Expanded Strict Fake-Canvas Tests:** Added 8 suites in `test/atari-bobs.test.mjs`, expanded `test/atari-pond-ripples.test.mjs` (`9 → 13` suites), and upgraded `test/ym-silicon-bg.test.mjs` (10 suites).
