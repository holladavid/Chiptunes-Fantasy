## Summary

This release builds on v1.6.1 with a major **Atari ST audio/visual showcase**: a v2.5 **"Director's Cut"** overhaul of the **`ym-bladerunner` Fantasy DSP core**, a full Winter-Gothic pixel-art redesign and DSE-rules compliance pass for **`AtariEnchantedCastle`**, beat-choreographed **IK+ martial-arts sprite rendering** inside **`AtariRetroSunset`** (`js/visuals/dse/atari/ik-fighter-sprites.js`), **fullscreen/mobile-landscape idle UI refinements**, the `V1.6.2` boot header update, and **`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`**.

## Changes

### 1. Atari ST YM2149F "Blade Runner" Core v2.5 (`js/worklets/atari/ym-bladerunner.js`)

- **TPT Zero-Delay Feedback filters (`ZDFFilter`):** Replaced `MoogFilter` across voices, noise, and Digidrum HPF with a Padé-[3/2] tan-approximated TPT SVF featuring soft cubic bandpass saturation and non-finite state recovery.
- **Authentic CS-80 voice model (`CS80Voice` × 3):** Implemented dual-rank detuned PWM/sawtooth VCOs with Nyquist gain roll-off, ~9 ms legato ribbon glide (preserving fast arpeggios and tracker slides 1:1), per-frame retrigger/arpeggio detection, 12 dB ZDF HPF -> 12 dB resonant ZDF LPF, **post-VCF pure Sine-lever addition**, and sample-rate-scaled VCA/envelope smoothing.
- **32-step envelope & YM6 MFP-timer FX (`decodeSlotFx`):** Added 32-step interpolated `YM_DAC` hardware envelopes, clean silencing on expired one-shot envelopes (`envStoppedZero`), PolyBLEP sawtooth buzzer anti-aliasing, and Slot 1/2 MFP-clocked decoding for **YM6 Digidrum playback rates (2.5–32 kHz)**, **Atari SID-Voice** modulation, and **TAO Sync-Buzzer** phase resets, plus a 1.5 ms anti-click Digidrum crossfade and start-flag snare-roll triggering.
- **Stereo space pipeline & telemetry:** Added `fastSinHalfPi` equal-power panning, a mono-compatible **True-Stereo Dimension-D BBD Chorus**, flutter-modulated **Tape Multi-Tap Delay**, **19 ms pre-delay + dual Allpass diffusers + 4-channel quadrature-modulated Lexicon 224 FDN Concert Hall Reverb**, asymmetric tape saturation, sample-rate-aware `DCBlocker`, signed block-peak visual output tracking, `resetFxAndVoices()` on `PLAY_TRACK`/`SEEK_TRACK`, and live `SET_PARAMS` (`reverbMix`, `delayMix`, `chorusMix`).

### 2. Atari ST DSE Upgrades (`enchanted-castle.js`, `ik-fighter-sprites.js`, `retro-sunset.js`)

- **`AtariEnchantedCastle` (`js/visuals/dse/atari/enchanted-castle.js`):**
  - Rebuilt into a dense 16-color, 9-bit Atari ST Shifter Winter Gothic Fortress (*Tanz der Vampire* × Caspar David Friedrich) strictly respecting `FLOOR_HORIZON_RATIO = 0.55`, portrait/ultrawide layouts, and `[0..width, 0..height]` clipping.
  - Removed DOM-dependent `applyOrderedDitherFade` and `ctx.globalAlpha`; added native **4×4 Bayer ordered-dither transitions (`drawBayerFadeCurtain`)** and Shifter sky seams (`drawShifterSkyDither`), `idle` resets, `stopping` guards, and 2.5 s `afterglow` Blood-Moon cooling.
  - Added 512-entry Dolomite/couloir & Carpathian ridge LUTs, a 64×64 weathered ashlar masonry & quoin LUT (`masonryLUT`), Bayer-dithered cylindrical towers (`drawCylindricalTower`), multi-volume fortress geometry (Keep, chimney, Z-occluded spire orbs, stair-turret, corner bartizan, Gatehouse with frieze/dormer/portcullis/postern, round bastion tower, East watchtower), sculpted 3D snow hill crossing `0.55 * h`, valley spruce groves, frozen brook, snow-fence, hollow-way trench, timber bridge, hunter silhouettes with lantern & footprints, tufted foreground spruces (`drawDetailedSnowSpruce`), 80-flake 3-layer winter snowfall, horizon aurora, forked spire lightning, double-ring parabolic fireworks, and YM `smooth[0..2]` channel choreography.
- **IK+ Sprite Catalog & Blitter (`js/visuals/dse/atari/ik-fighter-sprites.js`):**
  - Added a 100% DOM-free 9-bit Shifter sprite engine compiling 29 hand-crafted martial-arts keyframes into `Int16Array` RLE horizontal spans (`IK_SPRITE_FRAMES`, `drawCompiledSprite`) with zero render-loop allocations.
  - Supports dual archetypes (`karateka` & `kaempferin`) via `resolveFighterPalette`, shading smoothly from daylight into Zen moonlight `afterglow` within the 512-entry `ATARI_HEX_LUT`, alongside `drawGroundShadow`, animated trailing headband/ponytail physics (`drawFighterTrailingDetail`), and `drawStrikeSparks`.
- **`AtariRetroSunset` (`js/visuals/dse/atari/retro-sunset.js`):**
  - Integrated a 16-move IK+ choreography state machine (`MOVES`) with 50 Hz PAL timings, `canCancelAt` beat canceling, parabolic jumps (`FLYING_KICK`, `SPLIT_KICK`, `BACKFLIP`), opening/closing bows (`BOW_OPEN`, `BOW_CLOSE`), beatless courtyard footwork and `ZEN_KATA_A`/`ZEN_KATA_B` katas, per-fade-in archetype rolling (`_rollFighterOnFadeIn`) preserved across rotations and cleared on `idle`, Bonsai-safe `roamRadius` clamping, and foreground petals passing in front of the fighter.

### 3. Responsive Fullscreen Idle UI, Version & Governance (`index.html`, `css/style.css`, `js/app.js`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`)

- **Boot screen & governance (`index.html`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`):** Bumped `#boot-screen` header to `V1.6.2` and added Contributor Covenant v2.0 `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.
- **Fullscreen inactivity & mobile landscape rules (`css/style.css`, `js/app.js`):**
  - Added `#brand-logo` auto-hide on `#visual-zone.user-inactive` across native and pseudo-fullscreen, removed `#visual-zone.user-inactive #fs-ui-bar:focus-within` so clicked buttons do not pin the fullscreen bar visible, kept `#brand-logo` visible in windowed mobile landscape (`<= 1024×580`) via `:not(:fullscreen):not(:-webkit-full-screen):not(.pseudo-fullscreen)`, and restored explicit active `#fs-ui-bar` opacity in mobile landscape fullscreen.
  - Moved `isFS` evaluation inside the `mouseIdleTimer` timeout callback and bound `touchmove` plus `fullscreenchange` / `webkitfullscreenchange` listeners to `resetMouseIdleTimer()`.
